## ask about anything on your screen

an error you don't get, a setting you can't find, a form that won't send. circle it and ask in plain words. mallow sees your screen, answers in a short note beside your cursor, and marks the answer up in red pencil: it circles the button, underlines the line that matters, and flies over to point at it.

no copying error messages into a chat window. no describing where things are. you point, it points back.

<br>

## it walks you through it

<p align="center">
  <img src="media/walk.webp" width="100%" alt="in paint, the user asks how to resize an image. mallow circles the resize button and waits; when the dialog opens it guides them to 200%, ticks it, and confirms the new size with a magnifier.">
</p>

ask how to do something and you get **one step at a time**. mallow spotlights where to click, then waits. once you've done it, it notices on its own and shows the next step, pointing at where things are *now*. **i did it** and **stop** are there when you want them.

<br>

## do something with what you copied

<p align="center">
  <img src="media/copy.webp" width="100%" alt="on a french wikipedia page, the user copies a sentence and opens mallow. buttons slide up offering explain, summarize, translate and rewrite; they pick translate and the english appears in a card.">
</p>

copy some text, press the shortcut, and mallow offers to **explain**, **summarize**, **translate** or **rewrite** it. one click, no typing. it only offers: nothing is sent until you pick.

<br>

## it shows you, it doesn't just tell you

<p align="center">
  <img src="media/markups.webp" width="100%" alt="on a wikipedia page, mallow spotlights the opening paragraph, boxes the infobox, and puts numbered badges on the contents to show what to read first.">
</p>

mallow draws right on your screen, in a hand-drawn red pencil that fades when you move on.

| | |
|---|---|
| **circles, boxes, arrows** | the thing, the area, the direction |
| **highlighter** | under the line of text that matters |
| **spotlight** | dims everything but the part to look at |
| **numbered badges** | 1, 2, 3 for things in order |
| **labels** | names icons and unlabelled buttons |
| **ticks and crosses** | this one's right, not that one |
| **traced routes** | for drags and paths with a bend |
| **magnifier** | an enlarged copy of something too small to read |

<br>

## shortcuts

| | |
|---|---|
| <kbd>ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>space</kbd> | open or close mallow |
| drag · click · right-click | circle something · mark a spot · wipe your marks |
| <kbd>ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>z</kbd> | take back your last mark |
| <kbd>ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>alt</kbd>+<kbd>→</kbd> / <kbd>←</kbd> | next / previous message, from any app |
| <kbd>ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>c</kbd> | copy what mallow said (with nothing selected) |
| <kbd>ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>h</kbd> | everything mallow said before |
| <kbd>ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd> | settings |
| <kbd>esc</kbd> | close |

## get started

1. **[download mallow](../../releases/latest)** and run `mallow-setup-x.y.z.exe`. it installs just for you, no admin rights needed.
2. the setup window does the rest: it installs claude code if you don't have it yet and signs you in with your own claude account. be signed in to claude.ai in your browser first and it's one click.
3. do the 20-second practice round, then press <kbd>ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>space</kbd> whenever you want a hand.

**you need** windows 10 or 11 and a **claude pro or max** plan. mallow runs on your own plan through claude code: no api key, nothing extra to pay.

<br>

## your screen, your business

- mallow only looks at your screen **when you open it**, and the screenshot only leaves your computer with a question you ask.
- it's invisible to screen capture, so it never shows up in its own screenshots, your screen shares or your recordings.
- while it waits for you in a walkthrough, it compares tiny grey copies of your screen **on your computer**. they're never saved or sent.
- copied text is only sent when you pick an action, and single words like passwords are never offered.
- no accounts, no analytics. your settings and history stay in your windows profile.

## questions

<details>
<summary><b>windows says it doesn't recognise the app</b></summary>
<br>
mallow isn't code-signed yet, so smartscreen warns the first time. click <b>more info</b>, then <b>run anyway</b>.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>is this made by anthropic?</b></summary>
<br>
no. mallow is an independent app. it uses your own claude account through anthropic's claude code, which you install and sign in to yourself during setup.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>ctrl+space doesn't work</b></summary>
<br>
another app (often keyboard-language switching) may already use it. mallow then picks <kbd>ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>shift</kbd>+<kbd>space</kbd> and shows it in setup. you can change it in settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>it says my plan doesn't cover this model</b></summary>
<br>
some models need extra usage credits on some plans. pick another one in settings under <b>model</b>.
</details>

<summary><b>what's new?</b></summary>
<br>
every version's notes are on the <a href="../../releases">releases page</a>.
</details>
