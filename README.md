## Ask about anything on your screen

An error you don't understand, a setting you can't find, a form that won't send. Press **Ctrl+Space**, circle the thing you mean, and ask in plain words. mallow sees your screen, answers in a short note beside your cursor, and marks the answer up in red pencil: it circles the button, underlines the line that matters, and flies over to point at it.

No copying error messages into a chat window, no describing where things are. You point, it points back.

<br>

## It walks you through it

<p align="center">
  <img src="media/walk.gif" width="100%" alt="In Settings, mallow spotlights Personalisation and waits. When the user clicks it and the page changes, mallow circles the Dark option with a label saying this one. After the user picks it, mallow ticks it and says that's it.">
</p>

Ask how to do something and mallow gives you **one step at a time**. It spotlights where to click, then waits. When you've done it, it notices on its own and shows you the next step, pointing at where things are *now*. **I did it** and **Stop** are there if you want them.

<br>

## Do something with what you copied

<p align="center">
  <img src="media/copy.gif" width="100%" alt="The user selects a Dutch sentence in an email and copies it. When they open mallow, buttons slide up offering Explain, Summarize, Translate and Rewrite. They pick Translate and an English translation appears in a card.">
</p>

Copy some text, press the shortcut, and mallow offers to **explain**, **summarize**, **translate** or **rewrite** it. One click, no typing. It only offers, it never sends anything until you pick.

<br>

## It shows you, it doesn't just tell you

<p align="center">
  <img src="media/markups.gif" width="100%" alt="On a shop dashboard, mallow puts numbered red badges on three tiles to show what to look at first, labels two icons as Print and Export, ticks the orders tile and crosses out the returns figure.">
</p>

mallow draws right on your screen, in a hand-drawn red pencil that fades when you move on:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Circles, boxes and arrows** | for the thing, the area, the direction |
| **Highlighter** | under the line of text that matters |
| **Spotlight** | dims everything but the part to look at |
| **Numbered badges** | 1, 2, 3 for things in order |
| **Labels** | names icons and unlabelled buttons |
| **Ticks and crosses** | this one's right, not that one |
| **Traced routes** | for drags and paths with a bend |
| **Magnifier** | an enlarged copy of something too small to read |

## Get started

1. **[Download mallow](../../releases/latest)** and run `mallow-setup-x.y.z.exe`. It installs just for you, no admin rights needed.
2. The setup window takes it from there: it installs Claude Code if you don't have it yet and signs you in with your own Claude account. Being signed in to claude.ai in your browser first makes that one click.
3. Do the 20-second practice round, then press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> whenever you want a hand.

**You need** Windows 10 or 11 and a **Claude Pro or Max** plan. mallow runs on your own plan through Claude Code, so there's no API key and nothing extra to pay.

<br>

## Your screen, your business

- mallow only looks at your screen **when you open it**, and the screenshot only leaves your computer with a question you ask.
- It's invisible to screen captures, so it never shows up in its own screenshots, your screen shares or recordings.
- While it waits for you in a walkthrough, it compares tiny grey copies of your screen **on your computer**. Those are never saved or sent.
- Copied text is only sent when you pick an action, and single words like passwords are never offered.
- No accounts, no analytics. Your settings and history stay in your Windows profile.

## Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Windows says it doesn't recognise the app</b></summary>
<br>
mallow isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns the first time. Click <b>More info</b>, then <b>Run anyway</b>.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is this made by Anthropic?</b></summary>
<br>
No. mallow is an independent app. It uses your own Claude account through Anthropic's Claude Code, which you install and sign in to yourself during setup.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Ctrl+Space doesn't work</b></summary>
<br>
Another app (often keyboard-language switching) may already use it. mallow then picks <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> and shows it in setup. You can change it in settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>It says my plan doesn't cover this model</b></summary>
<br>
Some models need extra usage credits on some plans. Pick another one in settings under <b>Model</b>.
</details>
